# Spec 01: Playground Lobby

> Source: Transcribed from reMarkable notebook `Playground.pdf`

## Concept & Mechanics
- **Lobby Hub:** A stylized game selection hub where a player chooses a game.
- **3D Environment:** Simple 3D retro-looking playground modeled in Blender.
- **Equipment Selector:** Each piece of equipment on the playground represents a minigame.
- **Progression & Quests:** Players go on quests and accomplish goals to level up and progress.
- **Navigation:** Walk/explore through the 3D space in first or third person to approach equipment and select a game.
